💚 Health Tips

Health for the Woman Who Feeds the Nation

Practical, doctor-reviewed health tips specific to farming women — in the field and at home.

Pesticide Safety 🧤
Always Wear PPE While Spraying
Wear rubber gloves, a face mask and covered shoes while handling any pesticide. Wash hands, face and body with soap immediately after work. Never spray on empty stomach.
Source: ICAR Guidelines · Reviewed by Dr. Sunita Menon, AIIMS
Maternal Health 🤱
Iron & Folic Acid in Pregnancy
Pregnant women farmers need 27mg iron daily. Eat dark leafy greens, jaggery, and sesame seeds. Take free IFA tablets from your ASHA didi. Avoid heavy field lifting after Week 20.
Source: NHM India · WHO Recommendation
Heat Safety ☀️
Prevent Heat Stroke in the Field
Drink at least 1 glass water every 20 minutes while working. If dizzy or nauseous, sit in shade immediately. Mix salt + sugar in water as ORS. Avoid direct sun 11 AM–3 PM.
Source: Ministry of Health — Heat Action Plan 2025
Back Health 🙆‍♀️
Protect Your Back While Farming
Chronic back pain affects 78% of women farmers. Use long-handled tools where possible. Take a 10-minute break every hour. Do 5 minutes of simple back stretches morning and evening.
Source: ICMR Research 2023 · Physiotherapy Council India
Nutrition 🥗
Iron-Rich Foods for Women Farmers
62% of rural women farmers are anaemic. Eat bajra, horse gram, dark leafy greens and jaggery daily. Pair with Vitamin C (lemon, amla) for better iron absorption.
Source: NFHS-5 India · Nutrition Foundation of India
Mental Health 🧘‍♀️
You Are Not Alone — Your Mental Health Matters
Farming stress is real. If you feel overwhelmed, talk to AnnaSakhi, your ASHA worker, or call iCall: 9152987821. Joining a Mahila SHG group can significantly reduce isolation and stress.
Source: NIMHANS · iCall Helpline
